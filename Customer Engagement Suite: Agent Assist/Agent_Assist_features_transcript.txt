Agent Assist offers a robust suite of features to enhance the level of service
that your agents can provide your customers. Agent Assist features come into
action during particular stages of a conversation with an end-user. At the end
of a conversation between an end-user and a conversational agent, the
conversation is either ended, or there is an escalation to a human agent. During
this handoff stage, two Agent Assist features, summarization and sentiment
analysis, are used to extract information from the conversation, in order to
determine where the conversation should be directed, and to provide an overview
of the conversation for the human agent. And then, during the conversation
between an end-user and a human agent, there are Agent Assist features to
support the human agent navigate the conversation, utilizing internal documents
and data, along with generative AI capabilities, to enable a fast, efficient,
and successful interaction. These include, Smart Reply, Proactive Generative
Knowledge Assist, and Live Transcription. Of course, summarization and sentiment
analysis can also come into action at the end of the conversation between the
end-user and the human agent, to extract important information from that
interaction as well. Now let’s briefly introduce each of these features,
starting with the Agent Assist features for agent handoff. Summarization is
designed to improve agents’ post-call average handle time, and to get the human
agent up-to-speed with the conversation that the customer had with a virtual
agent. Generative AI can help summarize call scripts or emails on behalf of
agents and suggest their next steps. Agents would no longer need to type
outbound summaries for the next agent to follow up on, as the summaries can be
created automatically, improving processing times and responses. Furthermore,
summarization automates the creation of concise summaries, saving agents
valuable time and ensuring consistent record-keeping. With summarization, both
customers and developers can use predefined sections, or create custom sections.
Additionally, it can generate summaries for those common requests customers
usually have. This allows developers to write their own prompts to fit specific
business needs. It's not just for the end of a conversation either; you can
generate summaries anytime during the interaction. Agents are able to review
those partial summaries, and when they transfer a call, the next agent can take
note of what's already been summarized. Moreover, it even works for recapping
interactions that happened before the customer transfers to a live agent, such
as with IVR or a chatbot. All of this supports agents’ understanding of the
context better and reduces post-call time, resulting in smoother conversations.
Sentiment Analysis is a great way to better understand customer interactions at
a deeper level. It determines emotional intent of a conversation to understand
whether is positive, neutral or negative. Additionally, it can extract entities,
classify them and provide additional information on them. Sentiment is
represented by score and magnitude values, metrics that are returned in the
response. The score of the sentiment ranges between minus 1 (which is negative)
and 1 (which is positive), and it corresponds to the overall emotional leaning
of the text. The magnitude value indicates the overall strength of emotion (both
positive and negative) within the given text, between 0.0 and infinite. Next,
let’s explore the Agent Assist features that support a human agent during a
conversation with an end-user. First, there is Smart Reply. Smart Reply offers
chat support through streamlining chat interactions between your agents and
customers, by proposing accurate and contextually appropriate responses that are
trained on your data. Instead of manually typing complete answers to customers’
questions, agents can select an answer predicted by Smart Reply, that not only
saves their time, but the customer’s time as well. The next features are
Generative Knowledge Assist (or GKA), and Proactive Generative Knowledge Assist,
or PGKA. Generative Knowledge Assist is much like an encyclopedia for agents,
providing relevant and fast-retrieval information. This feature uses a data
store handler to offer relevant knowledge articles in response to agent
questions. Here is an example of where Generative Knowledge Assist can be used
during a conversation between the agent and the customer. In the conversation
that is happening between the agent and the customer, the customer asks Cymbal
Mobile, what available data plans are available when they travel. The agent can
take a moment to ask a question to GKA, to search for content in the Cymbal
Mobile data stores. For example, ‘Available data plans for travel abroad’. The
data store handler will return a link to a relevant document on Cymbal Mobile’s
available data plans for travel, in this case the Cymbal Mobile Travel Pass. The
agent can access the document and use that information to provide a quick and
efficient answer to the customer. Questions can be asked at any point during the
conversation, if the agent wants to reference a company document to support
their response. Generative Knowledge Assist ultimately supports the improvement
of key performance metrics such as average handle time, first call resolution
rate, and customer satisfaction, also known as CSAT. Proactive Generative
Knowledge Assist or PGKA, can be used to automatically generate potential
questions, or questions and answers during a conversation with a customer, to
help provide appropriate high-quality responses, which allow the agent to focus
on the customer. Proactive Generative Knowledge Assist focuses on two key
functionalities, the first being query generation. Typically, agents will be
listening or chatting with the customer, while trying to search for information
that they don’t have immediate answers for. With Agent Assist, it now has the
ability to analyze the context of the conversation to understand which
particular terms should generate the meaningful queries for the agent, similar
to what an agent will normally do in a contact centre. The second functionality
is answer generation. After the query is generated, the next step is for the
agent to find the most suitable solution. More often than not, agents are
required to filter through documentation, or visit various internal and external
websites in order for them to find the right solution. With answer generation,
this particular feature will generate the correct answers directly for the
agent. Moreover, it also provides the links and source information to where the
solution was generated from. In the event that agents decide that the answer
provided is not accurate, they still have the option to make edits before
sending or reading it to the customer. Alternatively, they can also look up the
source documentation to find the right answers. Here is an example of the Agent
Assist console where Proactive Generative Knowledge Assist is learning from the
ongoing conversation between the agent and the customer. During the conversation
between the agent and the customer, the customer asks about Cymbal Mobile’s
available data plans for travelling abroad. Proactive Generative Knowledge
Assist starts searching for content related to the customer query in the
company’s data stores. And provides the agent with proactive answers, as well as
any of the references that related to that particular turn of the conversation.
The agent can use this generated answer as part of their response to the
customer. This innovative tool utilizes the ongoing conversation as context to
proactively suggest relevant search queries, enhancing the overall efficiency of
the support process. With robust search capabilities that deliver generative
answers, agents can access information quickly and effectively. Additionally,
the system supports configuration for both internal and external knowledge
bases, ensuring that agents can find the right resources for each situation. It
also features internationalization support for 38 Gemini languages, making it
versatile for diverse user bases. Next, you’ll explore Live Transcription, a
valuable tool for enhancing communication and information retention. Live
Transcription functions similarly to captions, providing a real-time written
record of spoken dialogue which offers several benefits. The first benefit is
that it significantly improves an agent's comprehension of the conversation.
This is particularly helpful for non-native speakers who may struggle with rapid
or nuanced speech. Secondly, it also aids focus and memory, allowing agents to
process and recall essential details. Furthermore, it can be invaluable in
situations with less-than-ideal audio, such as noisy environments or when
dealing with hard-of-hearing individuals. Beyond immediate comprehension, live
transcription provides a readily accessible history of the call. This allows
agents to quickly review previous exchanges, helping them to clarify points and
ensure accuracy. An advantage of this is that the record can also be shared with
the customer as a helpful reference after the interaction. Finally, the
transcribed text can be stored within a Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
system. This creates a searchable archive of conversations, facilitating future
analysis, quality assurance, and a more comprehensive understanding of customer
interactions over time. Coming up, you’ll explore multi-lingual support through
real-time translation. With Real-time Translation, agents no longer have to be
concerned when their customers speak a different language. Google Cloud's Agent
Assist can auto-detect over a hundred languages in real time and instantly
transcribe and translate chats, making language barriers. a thing of the past!
It realizes that accuracy is important, and therefore it leverages the same
technology behind Google Translate and Translation AI products to ensure
seamless communication with customers worldwide. Furthermore, agents can observe
their own conversations whether through chat or transcription. This feature also
gives them an opportunity to observe the translations from a customer
communicating in a different language. Likewise, the customer will notice both
texts in both languages as well. This ultimately results in a better
understanding of the conversation between the agent and the customer. Multi-
lingual support through real-time translation helps improve various key elements
of a contact center environment. These include average handle time, agent
utilization rate and first contact resolution.
