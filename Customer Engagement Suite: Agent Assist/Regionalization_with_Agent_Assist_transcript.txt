Now that you have a better understanding of Agent Assist’s vision and the
features that support it, let’s dive into its regionalization aspects.
Regionalization provides data residency to keep customer’s data-at-rest
physically within a specified geographical region or location. This means that
when the customer specifies a region, their data-at-rest is not stored outside
that region. Regionalization is specially helpful in scenarios when your system
has regulatory or policy requirements that govern where your data must reside.
Additionally, where your network latencies might be improved when the data is in
the same region as your customers An important point to keep in mind is that the
Agent Assist regionalization and data residency does not apply to data-in-use or
data-in-transit. Keep in mind two limitations associated with the
regionalization when working with Agent Assist: The first is that model training
in Agent Assist does not support regionalization. This means that your data may
get routed outside the region during this process. The second limitation relates
to CES Transcription, which currently only supports multi-region data in use and
data at rest (if not using the Speech Adaptation feature) in the EU, US and
Canada. In this module, you learned about Agent Assist’s vision and its various
